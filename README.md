
# Orientation Tasks 1

## Task 1 - Simulation in Everyday Life

A restaurant is a good example where simulation can be used to study customer waiting times, food preparation, and service. Simulation helps us test different situations, like having more waiters or chefs, without disturbing real customers. It also saves time and money compared to testing in real life. For sustainability, simulation can help reduce food waste, save electricity, and use resources more efficiently. Overall, it helps restaurants improve their service and reduce unnecessary costs.

## Task 2 - Real-World Operational System

A restaurant is a good example of a real-world operational system. Variability means the number of customers changes throughout the day, and some orders take longer to prepare than others. Complexity means there are many activities happening at the same time, like taking orders, cooking food, serving customers, and cleaning tables. Interconnectedness means all these activities depend on each other. For example, if the kitchen is slow, waiters cannot serve food on time, and customers have to wait longer. Simulation can help the restaurant understand these problems and improve its service.
